1. The stage: TRINITY's cooling budget in one picture

Inside the energy-driven phase, TRINITY solves the Weaver-style bubble structure T(r) every step and splits the radiative loss by temperature zone:

  • L1 — the hot interior (T>105.5 K): shocked-wind gas in CIE. Radiates weakly; its emission scales with the bubble volume.
  • L2+L3 — the conduction front (104<T<105.5 K): the thin bubble-side layer draped on the contact discontinuity at R2. The interface surface is the CD — L3's outer edge against the shell's inner skin; Ṁ is mass ablated FROM the shell, transiting L3→ L2 into the hot bubble. L2+L3 is the radiative emission OF that front — dense, non-CIE, on the cooling-curve peak — and it scales with the CD area.

Two more players complete the budget: Lleak (bulk hot-gas escape through shell holes — the Rogers & Pittard channel; not radiation) and Lmech (the SB99 wind+SN input). The transition trigger watches the instantaneous cooling fraction

The problem the workstream solves: TRINITY's 1-D spherical interface under-counts the interface area — a real 3-D interface is fractal/corrugated (Lancaster 2021a,b), so 3-D sims measure Θ≈0.9-0.99 while TRINITY's native θ sits at 0.2–0.6 for most GMCs and the trigger never fires.

Where exactly is "the interface"? — the R2 contact discontinuity, NOT the L1–L2 boundary (code-verified; bubble_luminosity.py zone slices at :736/:752/:799, ICs at :392).

The solver sets its boundary at r2' = R2 - dR2 with T≈3×104 K (cold, the shell side) and integrates inward to R1, where T climbs past 105.5 K. Verified radial layout, inside → out:

  • The L1–L2 boundary is a thermometer reading, not a material boundary. The 105.5 K line (_CIEswitch) is only the CIE ↔ non-CIE cooling-table switch within one continuous fluid.
  • The physical interface is the CD at R2 — the only place two genuinely different media touch: hot tenuous shocked wind inside, cold dense swept-up shell outside.
  • Why the cooling lives in L2+L3: pressure is ≈uniform across the subsonic interior, so n∝1/T. Toward R2 the temperature falls → density rises, and the emissivity n2Λ(T) peaks exactly there. L1 is hot and rarefied → weak cooling, which is precisely why the classical Weaver interior is the energy-retaining reservoir.

So fA — Lancaster's fractal-area factor — multiplies that front and leaves L1 (bulk volume, wrong side) and Lleak (bulk escape, not radiation) alone. The numerical resolution map mirrors the physical one: L1 is radially enormous (~30% of R2) and gets most of the raw ~60k grid, but L2/L3 are razor-thin skins (~0.1 AU, ~104 km) that get their own dense resamples — 103–105× finer per pc. Measured in data/zone_resolution.csv.

2. The two knobs — which is which

fA — the source-term (in-ODE) boost: cooling_boost_fA

Why it acts on L2+L3: fA is the 1-D projection of the fractal area multiplier, Aeff=fA·4π R22. Extra interface area means proportionally more interface emission, and the layer that radiates it is the bubble-side conduction front.

The crucial consequence — the structure responds. Because the boost lives inside the ODE, the solved T(r) changes: the interface layer gets cooler and denser, the conductive evaporation flux falls (Ṁevap↓ — the El-Badry Eq-47 sign, measured across the theta5s matrix), and the solved Lcool genuinely rises. θ is an output of physics, not an imposed number.

fmix — the post-hoc multiplier: cooling_boost_mode='multiplier'

The multiplier is applied after get_bubbleproperties returns — the T(r) structure and the evaporation Ṁ are frozen at the unboosted solution; only the scalar fed to the energy budget and the trigger is inflated. It is the frozen-structure limit of fA. This asymmetry is physical, is verified in source, and is untouched by everything below.

3. The metric artifact, and the fix FINDINGS §17 → §18

Both knobs feed the same energy equation, and the campaign scored them with one window metric:

The trajectory CSVs store Lcool = the raw bubble_LTotal, and theta = bubble_Lloss/Lmech, which already carries whatever boost is active. The analysis integrated the wrong one:

Under mode='none' and under fA the two are identical (the fA boost is already inside bubble_LTotal), which is why no fA number moves. Under multiplier the effective loss is Lleak+fmixLcool, so the raw construction understates it by exactly fmix — verified as a ratio of exactly 8.000 on every fm8 row.

4. The corrected head-to-head the comparison REVERSES

The decision tree's own metric is band-entry-dose uniformity: the knob whose calibrated dose varies less across cloud density is the better single constant.

Where the grid under-brackets, band entry is estimated from the local log–log slope of the top two doses:

A global power law fitted on fmix≤4 (p=0.47/0.55/0.70 for bench3/2/1, R2≥0.99) is rejected: it predicts 1.12 for bench2 at fmix=8 where 0.895 was measured, i.e. the response saturates and that fit would understate the entry dose. So the inversion is a defensible estimate, not a measurement. Settling it needs fmix≈{12,16} on bench1/bench2 — about 4 arms.

The three original elimination legs, re-read. (1) "never reaches the band at fmix≤8" — true of the grid, false as a trend claim; the grid under-brackets exactly as fA's did at ≤16 before bench6 extended it. (2) "wrong-sign dose-response" — WITHDRAWN; the sign was the artifact. The in-text rationalization ("the frozen structure's Lcool collapses") correctly explains why the raw ratio falls, but the raw ratio is not the model's cooling fraction: the energy the run actually drains is fmixLcool. (3) "fm8 false-fires while Θcum≈0.10" — WITHDRAWN; corrected Θcum at fm8 is 0.77–0.90, and the residual fire-vs-band offset matches fA's own benign bench3 pattern (fires at 12, band at ≈16).

7. The §16 fallback double-boost — bounded out, not fixed §19

On a no-root segment the trigger reads _Lcool from params['bubble_Lloss'] — already the effective, boosted loss — and passes it through the boost again:

The loop does not break on a no-root segment (it continues to NO_ROOT_HANDOFF_STREAK = 50), so the check at :1296 is evaluated on that path — the bug is live and inflates the trigger's θ by exactly fmix. But this Lloss is a local variable, never written back, so Θcum is untouched.

and across all 120 arms, 0 carry a cooling_balance termination in metadata (every outcome is stopping_time, shell_collapsed or shell_dissolved). So meta_fired is false everywhere and every fire label in the whole map, both knobs, is computed from the single-boosted θmax — not from the trigger event. A double-boost-induced early fire cannot manufacture a FIRED label on its own.

Residual exposure and its direction: the double-boost can still end the energy phase early, truncating the window θmax and Θcum are measured over — a false-negative direction, the opposite of the fm-flattering bias originally feared. Decision: do not apply the fix now. It is a trigger-path production edit carrying the full equivalence ladder, and it would change no published number (default is none, where the re-application is the identity; SC-0 failed so nothing ships). §16 stays registered as a live latent bug for whoever next runs multiplier arms and reads the fire event from the run rather than from θmax.

This also corrects a wording error propagated across the workstream: "FIRE = the run actually fired the trigger (STRICTER than θmax≥0.95)" is backwards — the column is the θmax criterion. Fixed in place in FINDINGS §15e/§15h, SOURCE_TERM_DESIGN §3 and PLAN.md (×2).

8. SC-0 — the state-coupled fA screen: FAIL (unaffected by all of the above)

Phase 6 asked whether the fitted fA(n̄) could be replaced by a derived law. Three candidates were screened offline against measured targets. The review verified SC-0 is untouched by the metric artifact — its targets are fA-side doses only.

The discriminator is the spread of (predicted / measured) across arms — calibration-invariant, so a law with the right shape scores ≈1 even if offset, while a wrong shape scatters.

C2 is falsified, and not by a units bug. L21b Eq 13 with TRINITY's own non-CIE cooling table gives tcool≈0.03 yr in peak-cooling gas, hence ℓcool≈8×10-15 pc — and the result is robust to the cascade index p: p=0/0.3/0.5/0.7 give 2.9×10-7/1.7×10-10/8.5×10-15/7.6×10-25 pc. For every p<1, ℓcool lies below every physical and numerical scale in the problem, so Eq 11's operative ℓ is set by the truncation scale, not by cooling. The unit cross-check passed exactly.

C3's result is the most consequential: the fitted scalar looked excellent (1.9×) on the three benches it was fitted to and collapses to 56× on the wider suite — direct evidence that fA(n̄) is a local fit, not a law. SC-0's FAIL is the pre-registered stop: SC-1 onward do not proceed, and no production code is written.

9. Where this leaves the decision

Unchanged. Production default stays cooling_boost_mode=none. fmix stays retained as an opt-in fallback and as the bench harness's control arm. SC-0's FAIL is terminal, so no fA form ships. Every fA number in the record (band entry 13.9/53.5/74.8, thresholds 1→4→12→24→64, the p=3.33 collapse law) is re-verified bit-stable.
Withdrawn. "fmix eliminated by measurement", and all three of its legs.
Reversed. On band-entry uniformity fmix (2.96×) is better than fA (5.39×). Two caveats bound the weight: fA's spread is fully measured while fmix's is ⅔ extrapolated, and both knobs' Θcum carry a large frozen share that is worse for fA.
What still favours fA, untouched: the physical asymmetry. fA acts inside the structure ODE so the structure responds and Ṁ falls (El-Badry Eq-47 sign, theta5s-measured); fmix leaves the structure frozen by construction. This was always the stronger argument, and it is the one the corrected data leaves standing. The empirical calibration argument that the head-to-head was meant to settle now points the other way — so it should be dropped from the case for fA, not reversed into a case for fmix.

The one premise that needs an explicit decision: the R0→R2 retirement ladder. It was justified by "fmix was eliminated as a calibration knob". That justification is gone. The ladder is already halted permanently at R0 by SC-0's failure, so nothing operational hangs on it — but the stated reason should either be re-derived from the physical asymmetry (recommended) or withdrawn.
